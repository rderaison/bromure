# From Your Phone

Once two or more sessions are running, a **Switchboard** row appears at the top of the Sessions list: one conversation that keeps track of all the others. It tells you what's working, what's stuck and what needs you; it carries your answers to the right session; and it can start, resume or put away sessions for you.

You can also reach it from your phone, over **Signal** or **WhatsApp**:

- *"what's going on?"* — a short status of every session in flight
- *"api 2"* — answer a blocked session's question (it quotes the question and numbers the options for you)
- *"start a codex session in infra to bump the coturn image"* — new work
- **STOP** — the Switchboard stops acting on any session at once (it still answers questions); **RESUME** turns it back on

The Switchboard never approves a permission prompt or answers a blocked session on its own: it only does so when you asked, and Bromure checks that your own words ask for it.

## Setting it up

Open **File › Infrastructure › Signal / WhatsApp Connector…** and click **Set Up the Connector**. Bromure creates a small machine of its own for it (listed under **Messaging** in the sidebar, with the other infrastructure). Your messaging account's keys live on that machine and nowhere else. It starts with Bromure and suspends when you quit, like a registry.

Then click **Connect…** on Signal or WhatsApp and pick **where you want to chat with Bromure**:

### Chat with yourself

Bromure becomes a linked device of your own account, and you talk to it in your **"Message yourself"** chat (WhatsApp) or **"Note to Self"** (Signal).

- Nothing else to set up: scan the QR code with your phone (**Settings › Linked devices › Link a device**). WhatsApp also offers a pairing code if scanning isn't practical.
- **Replies don't notify you** — WhatsApp and Signal never notify you about your own chat. Open it to see them. (Bromure's replies start with ☎️.)
- Only that chat reaches the Switchboard. A linked device receives your other conversations too (that's how linking works): they wait a few seconds in the connector's inbox, then are dropped — they never reach the Switchboard, and its log records only that a message was ignored, not what it said.

### Chat with a Bromure contact

Bromure uses a **second account** and shows up in your chats like a person, so its replies **notify you** like any message.

**Signal** — you need a spare phone number that can receive one text or one call:

1. Enter the spare number and click **Text Me a Code**.
2. If Signal asks for a captcha, open its captcha page, solve it, right-click **Open Signal**, copy the link and paste it in.
3. If no text arrives (landlines, some VoIP numbers), click **Call Me Instead** — Signal reads the code out. (Signal only calls a number it has tried to text first.)
4. Enter the code, and your **own** Signal number: Bromure only answers messages from it.

**WhatsApp** — Bromure can't create a WhatsApp account; it links to one you set up for it:

1. Get a spare number: a second SIM or eSIM, or a landline (WhatsApp can verify it with a voice call).
2. Install **WhatsApp Business** — it runs next to your regular WhatsApp on the same phone — and register it with the spare number.
3. In Bromure, enter your **personal** WhatsApp number (Bromure only answers it), then scan the QR code with **WhatsApp Business** (**Settings › Linked devices**).

Then write to that contact from your personal WhatsApp.

> **WhatsApp note:** WhatsApp doesn't officially support linking a device this way, and may disconnect it. Signal is the more dependable channel.

## Checking it works

After connecting, **Send a Test Message**. With *Chat with yourself* it lands silently in your own chat — open it to check. The connector's **Log** (at the bottom of its window) records every message sent and every message ignored, without their content.

## Privacy and security

- Only you reach the Switchboard: your own number (a Bromure contact) or your own self-chat. Anything else is dropped by the connector before the Switchboard could see it; the log notes that a message was ignored, never its content.
- Your messaging keys stay on the connector's machine; the rest of Bromure never holds them. Removing the connector deletes them.
- What the Switchboard sends you is only what it chooses to relay; credentials inside your workspaces are stand-ins, never the real secrets.
